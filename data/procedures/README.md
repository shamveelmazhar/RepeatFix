# Procedure chunks

All `*.jsonl` files here are loaded by `ProcedureIndex.load()` and searched together. Each line is one chunk: `ac_number` (document number), `chapter`, `chapter_title`, `section`, `section_title`, `paragraph`, `title`, `page` (PDF page), `page_label` (printed page) and `text`. They were generated by `python -m app.procedures build` and `python -m app.handbooks` (see `backend/README.md`). Don't edit them by hand.

| File | Document | Split by | Cited as |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ac_43-13-1b.jsonl` | FAA AC 43.13-1B CHG 1, *Acceptable Methods, Techniques, and Practices: Aircraft Inspection and Repair* (editorial update, May 2024) | paragraph (`9-14. TIRE INSPECTION AND REPAIR`) | `AC 43.13-1B, Chapter 9, Section 1, par. 9-14 (Tire Inspection and Repair), page 9-12` |
| `faa-h-8083-30b.jsonl` | FAA-H-8083-30B, *Aviation Maintenance Technician Handbook – General* | PDF bookmark heading | `FAA-H-8083-30B, Chapter 1 (Safety, Ground Operations, & Servicing), "Hot Start", page 1-17` |
| `faa-h-8083-31b.jsonl` | FAA-H-8083-31B, *Aviation Maintenance Technician Handbook – Airframe* (both volumes) | PDF bookmark heading | `FAA-H-8083-31B, Chapter 13 (Aircraft Landing Gear Systems), "Bleeding Shock Struts", page 13-14` |
| `faa-h-8083-32b.jsonl` | FAA-H-8083-32B, *Aviation Maintenance Technician Handbook – Powerplant* | PDF bookmark heading | `FAA-H-8083-32B, Chapter 7 (Propellers), "Hunting & Surging", page 7-30` |

For handbook chunks, `section` is empty, `section_title` holds the parent headings (`Landing Gear Types > Shock Struts`) and `paragraph` is a section key (`13.14`) used only to group the parts of one section; it is not printed in the book. Figure captions on a page are appended to the section that holds most of that page, as `(Figure 13-26: ...)`. Glossaries and indexes are not included.

All four are US Government works in the public domain. Sources:
- AC 43.13-1B: https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/advisory_circulars/index.cfm/go/document.information/documentID/99861
- AMT Handbooks: https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/handbooks_manuals/aviation
